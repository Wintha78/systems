Based on the sources, the **Advanced Thinker** represents **Stage 5** in the six stages of cognitive development. This stage is defined by a significant milestone: the individual begins to **notice tangible changes** in their thought patterns and how they process information.

### Characteristics of the Advanced Thinker

In the larger context of the **Stages of Improvement**, the Advanced Thinker has progressed through the earlier phases of awakening and discipline to reach a point where their efforts are yielding visible results.

- **Tangible Transformation:** Unlike the "Practicing Thinker" (Stage 4), who is primarily focused on the discipline of regular practice, the Advanced Thinker is actively observing a **shift in their mental architecture**.
- **Conscious Evolution:** Reaching this stage is a **gradual process** that requires a "conscious commitment to learning" and the persistence to navigate through "plateaus of learning" where progress may feel slow.
- **Acceptance of Flaws:** Advanced thinkers have moved beyond the state of the "Unreflective Thinker" (Stage 1) by **accepting specific problems** in their own thinking and putting in the hard work to improve themselves.

### Tools and Strategies for Advancement

The sources suggest that thinkers at this level use specific tools to maintain their progress and prepare for the final stage of becoming consistently insightful.

- **Intellectual Journaling:** This is a vital tool for the Advanced Thinker to analyze significant emotional situations, record their responses, and determine **what they could do differently** in the future to ensure growth.
- **Internalizing Standards:** Individuals at this stage systematically monitor their thoughts against **intellectual standards** such as clarity, precision, and accuracy.
- **Character Reshaping:** There is a focus on cultivating **intellectual traits** like humility, empathy, and perseverance, which involves noticing when arrogance or defensiveness prevents them from learning something new.
- **Cognitive Monitoring:** An Advanced Thinker is more adept at engaging **System 2 thinking** (the slow, analytical mind) to catch and correct the "unforced errors" and biases produced by the fast, automatic **System 1**.

Ultimately, the Advanced Thinker is a **"participating actor"** who understands that excellence in thinking is a lifelong skill, similar to a physical discipline, and continues to put in the **conscious effort** required to reach a level of consistent insight.
